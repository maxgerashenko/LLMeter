Suite: local-coding-v1, ten short Python tasks with hidden tests and partial credit (easy weight 1, medium 2, hard 3). Score is the weighted share of hidden tests passed, 0 to 100. Tokens/sec is the average generation speed across the run's tasks. Limits per task: 4,000 output tokens and 5 minutes with thinking off; 12,000 tokens (thinking included) and 15 minutes with thinking on. An answer cut off at a limit scores 0, so a low thinking-on score can mean the model spent its budget on thinking, not that its answer was wrong. Results are read from data/local-runtime-results.csv (one row per task) and the model list from data/local-test-models.csv; a row marked planned, not installed or cannot run has no numbers; the reason a model cannot run is in the model list.
