Anthropic IPO: who holds the shares, and when they can sell

The IPO was 980 million shares at $83, about $81.3B: Anthropic sold 956,351,354 and existing holders sold 23,648,646. Each bar is one holder group's share of the company, in %, and a full bar is 100%; the small number under it is its value at the first-day close of $97.17 (Friday's close could not be found). Bars are tiered by when the group's shares are fully free to sell: Tier 1 is free now, Tier 2 by May 17, 2027, Tier 3 by Nov 18, 2027. Dates are the latest dates and can move earlier if earnings are published first. Bars drawn paler are rough estimates.

Caveats

  • Percentages do not add to 100%. The groups listed are about 95% of the company and are not a complete cap table.
  • Several figures are rough estimates: the private placement, employees and officers, and other big investors (marked "estimate"). Options and unvested shares are not counted.
  • "Fully free" dates are latest dates. They move earlier if earnings are published first. Employees could sell 10% from Nov 19, 2026 and another 35% shortly after FY2026 results. For the co-founders 29% is free at May 17, 2027.
  • Amazon and Google release dates are not confirmed. Nov 18, 2027 is the likely date.
  • No price-paid-before-the-IPO column: there is no reliable cost basis. The last private round was Series H at a $965B valuation.
  • Values use the first-day close of $97.17 and will move with the share price.
