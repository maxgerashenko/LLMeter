# Resources

Every public source LLMeter uses. Each entry has a link, who published it, when it was read, and a short note on what it is useful for. How these sources get their own information is traced in [SOURCE_GRAPH.md](SOURCE_GRAPH.md).

| # | Source | Publisher | Published | Read | What it gives LLMeter |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [LLM Sizer](https://www.theaibridges.com/tools/llm-sizer?s=1%7Egmac-studio-m5-ultra*96*512%7Ermlx%7Evmachine*mac-studio-m5-ultra%253A256) | The AI Bridge (built by Alex Grankin) | Live tool, early build | 2026-11-17 | Which open models fit a given machine (the link is set to a Mac Studio M5 Ultra with MLX) and estimated tokens per second. The formula is published ([methodology](https://www.theaibridges.com/tools/llm-sizer/about)) and the data is open ([llm-sizer-data](https://github.com/alexgrankinukr-hash/llm-sizer-data)). Useful for the hardware and local-inference side of progress. |
| 2 | [Artificial Analysis](https://artificialanalysis.ai/) | Artificial Analysis | Live leaderboard (Intelligence Index v5.0.1 when read) | 2026-11-17 | Independent comparison of models and API providers on quality, price, output speed and latency, plus indexes for coding agents, cyber and decision making. [Methodology](https://artificialanalysis.ai/methodology). Useful for benchmark and cost trends over time. |
| 3 | [Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Tasks](https://metr.org/blog/2025-03-19-measuring-ai-ability-to-complete-long-tasks/) | METR (Kwa, West, Becker et al.) | 2025-03-19 | 2026-11-17 | Introduces the "time horizon": the length of task, measured in expert human time, that an AI agent finishes 50% of the time. Finds it doubled about every 7 months from 2019 to 2025. Full paper: [arXiv 2503.14499](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14499); current numbers on [METR Time Horizons](https://metr.org/time-horizons/). Useful as a single long-run measure of agent capability. |
| 4 | [How Many Agents Make an Engineer](https://github.com/maxgerashenko/modernMBA/blob/main/research/ai-developer-replacement-usa.md) | Max Gerashenko (modernMBA repo) | 2026 (inputs dated up to November 2026) | 2026-11-17 | Max's own research note on the pure cost of running AI coding agents on owned GPU racks (GB200 NVL72 base case, about $6.38 per agent-hour) against US engineer pay from L1 to L7, built on BLS payroll data and METR time horizons. It is analysis, not primary data: use the sources it cites for any facts. |
